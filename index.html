About Andrea

Service, discipline,
and advocacy.

Andrea is a proud U.S. Army veteran and retired federal worker who comes from a long line of men and women dedicated to service, a family tradition of military service that shaped who she is and how she leads.

With a legal background and decades of experience navigating complex systems on behalf of everyday people, Andrea brings a rare combination of discipline, expertise, and advocacy to the table.

After years of living and traveling the world, Andrea chose Jacksonville and District 8 as home. With fresh eyes shaped by that experience, she sees clearly what so many long-time City Hall insiders have stopped noticing: Jacksonville is chronically under-invested.

Andrea is not a career politician. She's a veteran, a public servant, a legal mind, and a woman who has seen what thriving communities look like and knows District 8 deserves nothing less.

Why Now

I'm running because District 8 has been overlooked for too long. Our neighborhoods deserve safe streets, quality schools, and economic opportunities that keep our families here, not displaced.

I've watched our community fight for the basics: working stoplights, clean parks, and responsive city services. Families across this district are tired of being promised change that never comes.

What I'll Do

I'm running to change that. District 8 deserves someone who will walk through your neighborhood, sit at your table, and go to bat for you in City Hall every single day.

Priorities

The issues that matter most
to our community.

★

Public Safety & Community Policing

Advocate for community-based policing strategies that build trust between law enforcement and residents, with adequate police presence and lighting across District 8.

Education & After-School Programs

Fight for fully funded schools and expanded after-school and summer programs that give District 8 children a safe place to learn and grow.

Infrastructure & Clean Neighborhoods

Push for road repairs, park improvements, and consistent city services that every neighborhood deserves, not just the ones with the loudest political connections.

Health Equity & Mental Health Access

Champion affordable healthcare access and mental health resources for District 8 families, especially veterans and seniors.

Economic Development & Jobs

Attract businesses and workforce development programs that create living-wage jobs right here in District 8 and keep our dollars in our community.

Affordable Housing

Stand against displacement and fight for policies that keep District 8 families in the homes and neighborhoods they've built.
